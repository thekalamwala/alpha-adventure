This page follows the packing lists we send to travellers once they register. Pick your trip type and your season, and the list changes to match. Tick items off as you pack, and print a copy if you like. Items needed only in some seasons carry a note in brackets, for example (Required in winter). Items marked Recommended or Optional are our extra suggestions on top of the list we send you.

Pack for the trip you are taking, not for every "what if". Every extra item is weight you carry up and down the trail. If your trek leader or trip captain sends a list for your specific trip, follow that one first.

Summer and Pre Monsoon is March, April, May and early June. Monsoon is mid June, July, August and September. Post Monsoon is October and November. Winter is December, January and February. If your dates fall between two seasons, ask us before you buy anything.
